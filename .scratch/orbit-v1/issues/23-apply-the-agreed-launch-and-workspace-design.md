# 23 — Apply the agreed launch and workspace design

**What to build** Make the workspace and its opening sequence match the approved Orbit design.

**Blocked by** [02 — Reopen two separate saved account sessions](02-reopen-two-separate-saved-account-sessions.md), [13 — Update Home when a report is saved](13-update-home-when-a-report-is-saved.md), [19 — Resume an Agent conversation](19-resume-an-agent-conversation.md)

**Status** Complete

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The left sidebar smoothly collapses and expands. It and the right Agent panel use subtle near-white frost and softly squared corners. The Agent panel starts collapsed.
- [x] Orbit screens use a near-black charcoal center, with a single faint orbital arc at the edge of Home. Live sites stay crisp inside a solid frame.
- [x] On the near-white blurred launch screen, the mark and wordmark rise into view in a stagger, then lift and fade as Home becomes usable. Reduced motion opens a ready workspace directly.

## Verification

Inspect the Windows app at normal and reduced motion. Check contrast, focus, tab and panel collapse, and a live site's readability.

## Audit evidence

The Home polish checks verified the launch reveal, reduced motion, panel reversal, focus return, and the charcoal workspace with a faint orbit arc.
