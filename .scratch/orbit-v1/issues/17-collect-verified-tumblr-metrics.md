# 17 — Collect verified Tumblr metrics

**What to build** Run the on-demand report with permitted Tumblr activity from a saved source.

**Blocked by** [11 — Save an on-demand weekly report](11-save-an-on-demand-weekly-report.md)

**Status** Local capture ready · authorized source needed

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Keep blog follower and activity figures tied to the measured blog and reporting period.
- [x] Keep the source, date, and scope for each reading. Show the applicable carried-forward or unavailable state when the source cannot verify a figure.

## Verification

Verify one eligible live reading for Tumblr and compare the evidence and saved source scope. Simulate a missing metric with a source fixture.

## Audit evidence

Verified figures can be recorded against the saved Tumblr account and measured blog with an evidence link and period. Verify the live blog figures with the owner’s authorized source.
