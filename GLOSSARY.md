# Orbit

A local desktop workspace for managing social accounts, planned posts, and engagement reports.

## Language

**Platform**

A supported social site. The first version covers Facebook, Reddit, X, and Tumblr.

**Saved account**

A login on one platform that appears in the account picker. A platform can have multiple saved accounts.

**Publishing destination**

The exact place a post is intended to appear, such as a Facebook Page or Group, a subreddit, a Tumblr blog, or an X profile. A scheduled post identifies the saved account as well as the publishing destination.

**Draft**

Shared caption text and media for a planned post. Each publishing destination can have its own wording, including a title when the platform uses one.

**Prepared draft**

A draft ready for the user to finish publishing through their signed-in session. It can appear on the Calendar at a planned time.

**Scheduled post**

A post queued for automatic publishing to one destination, using a specific saved account and publish time. It contains the caption and media selected at scheduling time, and later changes are made on the scheduled post.

**Publishing result**

The outcome of publishing a post to one destination.

**Publishing attempt**

One try to publish a scheduled post under its selected account and destination.

**Verified publication**

A post confirmed live at its publishing destination, with a verified link and publish time.

**Verify publication**

The status of a publishing attempt whose outcome is still uncertain after the workspace checks the destination.

**Overdue post**

A scheduled post whose publish time passed while the workspace was closed. It is available to publish now or reschedule.

**Calendar entry**

A prepared draft, scheduled post, or verified publication displayed at its planned or actual publish time.

**Session**

A saved account's signed-in state on its platform. Social tabs for that account share the session.

**Account tab**

An open page belonging to one saved account. A saved account can have several account tabs that share its session.

**Active tab**

The account tab displayed in Orbit's center workspace. A chat request about the current website refers to this tab and its saved account.

**Agent connection**

The sign-in method Orbit uses to run its Agent. A saved social account supplies the separate identity used on that platform.

**Agent panel**

The collapsible chat alongside the active account tab or Orbit screen, where the user talks to the Agent.

**Agent browser**

The browser view the Agent uses for a task in a saved account's session. The Agent panel shows that view live, and the user can browse separately in the center workspace.

**Engagement snapshot**

A dated observation of a platform's engagement metrics, with the source and time of collection.

**Metric scope**

The measurement and reporting subject that make figures comparable, including the unit and coverage where relevant. For example, X post views and X reply views have separate scopes.

**Engagement baseline**

The previously verified engagement metrics used to compare figures for a saved account. The first saved report establishes the initial baseline.

**Headline metric**

A metric pinned to the top of Home for quick reference.

**Period total**

A metric total measured over a reporting window, such as post views during a dated period.

**Point-in-time count**

A metric total measured at a specific time, such as a verified follower or server member count.

**Report run**

An on-demand collection of engagement figures that can produce a saved weekly engagement report.

**Weekly engagement report**

An on-demand comparison of current engagement metrics against the engagement baseline. It groups evidence by platform and gives the last verified date for figures carried forward.
