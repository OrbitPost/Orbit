# Schedule posts for explicit destinations

Each scheduled post identifies both the saved account and the exact publishing destination so the calendar shows who will post and where it will appear. The calendar supports preparing posts across Facebook, Reddit, X, and Tumblr, with automatic publishing through authorized integrations and prepared drafts where the user finishes publishing. When the workspace reopens after a missed publish time, it marks the post as overdue and offers Publish now and Reschedule.
